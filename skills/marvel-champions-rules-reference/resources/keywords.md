# Marvel Champions — Keywords

**Source:** Rules Reference v1.7 | **Referenced by:** SKILL.md

---

## GENERAL KEYWORD RULES

- A keyword is an attribute that conveys specific rules to its card.
- If a card **gains multiple instances** of a keyword, additional instances have no effect **unless** that keyword is followed by a number (e.g., Hinder X, Retaliate X) — in which case the numbers are **added together**.
- Gained keywords are **not** considered to be printed on the card.
- Lost keywords are **still** considered to be printed on the card; cannot be regained while the ability causing the loss is in effect.
- Keywords have **timing priority over "Response" abilities** (see timing.md).

---

## KEYWORD REFERENCE

---

### ALLIANCE

When a player declares their intention to play a card with the alliance keyword, **any player(s) may help pay the costs** for that card.

- Only the **player playing the alliance card** is considered to be resolving it.

---

### ASSAULT

When a character makes a **basic thwart against a scheme** with the assault keyword, that character uses its **ATK instead of THW**.

- If the thwarting character is an ally, it takes **consequential damage listed under ATK** (not THW) after the thwart.
- Abilities that increase a character's "basic power" can increase that character's ATK when thwarting a scheme with assault.

---

### FORM

A card with the form keyword **grants an identity a unique form** in addition to the standard hero and alter-ego forms.

- These additional forms have their own conditions for changing into them.
- Changing into an additional form does **not** count against the once-per-turn flip limit, but **does** count as changing form for triggering card effects.
- Cards with "[type] form only" can only be played or put into play by a player whose identity is in the specified form.

---

### GUARD

While a minion with the **guard** keyword is engaged with a player, that player **cannot use cards they control to attack the villain** (a villain without this keyword).

- Guard only restricts attacks against the **villain**; the player can still attack other enemies.
- Due to the simultaneous nature of certain abilities (e.g., Wasp's Giant form), guard applies even if the guard minion would be removed from play during the same basic attack.

---

### HINDER X

When a card with the **hinder X** keyword enters play, **place X threat on that card**. (X is the value next to the keyword.)

---

### INCITE X

When a player **reveals** a card with the **incite X** keyword, **place X threat on the main scheme**. (X is the value next to the keyword.)

- If a villain flips to a new face (is revealed), and that face has incite X via a card effect, the incite resolves.

---

### LINKED (CARD TITLE)

Cards with the **linked** keyword **cannot be included in any deck**.

- They are **set aside at the start of the game** if any deck includes the card named in the parentheses following the keyword.
- Linked cards do **not** count toward minimum or maximum deck size.
- The number of linked cards set aside = the number included in the product from which the linked card came.
  - If multiple decks contain the named card: set aside the appropriate number of cards for **each deck**.
- When a player **takes control** of a linked card, that player becomes the **owner** of that card.

---

### OVERKILL

If an ally or minion takes any amount of **excess damage** from an attack with the overkill keyword:

- **Ally** → simultaneously deal damage equal to the excess amount to the **identity of the player who controls the ally**.
- **Minion** → simultaneously deal damage equal to the excess amount to the **villain**.

Additional rules:

- Overkill damage to an identity or villain is considered **damage from an attack** but does **not** constitute an attack against that character.
- If a card ability counts excess damage dealt, it counts the **same value** calculated when resolving overkill.
- When an ally or minion with a **tough status card** is dealt excess damage by an overkill attack → overkill damage is **not** dealt to the identity or villain.

---

### PATROL

While a minion with the **patrol** keyword is engaged with a player, that player **cannot use cards they control to thwart the main scheme**.

- Patrol only restricts thwarting the **main scheme**; the player can still thwart side schemes.
- Due to the simultaneous nature of certain abilities (e.g., Wasp's Giant form), patrol applies even if the patrol minion would be removed from play during the same basic thwart.

---

### PERIL

While a player is resolving a card with the **peril** keyword:

- That player **cannot consult** other players.
- Other players **cannot play cards or trigger abilities**.

---

### PERMANENT

A card with the **permanent** keyword cannot be defeated, leave play, or have any part of its text box blanked, **except by card abilities in the same set** (hero set, scenario set, or modular set).

- Permanent cards are **set aside before step 1 of setup** and are put into play later by abilities on other cards.
- Permanent cards do **not** count toward a player's minimum or maximum deck size.
- Permanent cards are **not valid targets** for card effects that would cause them to leave play.
  - If a permanent card would be targeted by such an effect (e.g., "discard the lowest-cost support you control"), that effect instead targets the **non-permanent card** fitting its criteria.
- If the card to which a **permanent attachment** was attached leaves play: resolve that attachment's "attach to" text. If it has none or no valid target: **remove the attachment from the game**.
- If a player is **eliminated** while a non-attachment permanent card they **do not own** is in their play area: remove that permanent card from the game.

---

### PIERCING

An attack with the **piercing** keyword **discards any tough status cards** from the attacked character **before** dealing damage.

- If a piercing attack would deal **no damage** to the attacked character: it does **not** discard tough status cards.

---

### QUICKSTRIKE

After a minion with the **quickstrike** keyword **engages** a player whose identity is in **hero form**, that minion **immediately attacks** that player.

- Quickstrike triggers on engagement; keywords have timing priority over "Response" abilities triggered at the same time (e.g., Widow's Bite).

---

### RANGED

An attack with the **ranged** keyword **ignores the retaliate keyword**.

- Ranged does not ignore guard or any other keyword — only retaliate.

---

### REQUIREMENT (RESOURCES)

A card with the **requirement** keyword **cannot be played** unless each resource of the specified type is spent while paying for that card's cost.

- **Cannot** be played "ignoring its resource cost" because the required resources cannot be paid.
- If the card's resource cost is reduced to 0, the requirement still must be met unless no actual resources need to be spent.

---

### RESTRICTED

A player **cannot have more than two restricted cards** in play under their control at the same time.

- A player can play or put into play a restricted card even if they already control two restricted cards.
- If a player ever controls more than two restricted cards in play: they must **immediately** choose and discard down to two.

---

### RETALIATE X

After a character with the **retaliate X** keyword is **attacked**, deal **X damage** to the attacker. (X = value next to the keyword.)

- The character with retaliate X must be **in play after the attack resolves** to deal this damage.
- Ranged attacks ignore retaliate.
- **Resolution order:** "after [character] takes any amount of damage..." abilities → Retaliate X → "after [character] is attacked..." abilities.
- When an attack targets multiple enemies: each attacked enemy with retaliate X that is **still in play** after the attack resolves deals its retaliate damage to the attacking character.

---

### SETUP

A card with the **setup** keyword **begins the game in play**.

- Put into play during the "Put Setup Cards Into Play" setup step.

---

### STALWART

A character with the **stalwart** keyword **cannot be stunned or confused**.

- If a character **gains** the stalwart keyword while having stunned and/or confused status cards: those status cards are **immediately removed**.

---

### STEADY

A character with the **steady** keyword can have **one additional stunned status card** and **one additional confused status card**.

- That character is **not stunned** unless it has **two** stunned status cards.
- That character is **not confused** unless it has **two** confused status cards.
- After that character's attack, scheme, or thwart is **canceled by a status card effect**: remove **all status cards of the corresponding type** from that character.

---

### SURGE

After a player **reveals** a card with the **surge** keyword, that player reveals an **additional encounter card** from the top of the encounter deck.

- Complete the process of resolving the original card, as well as any response abilities triggered by that card being revealed, **before** revealing the additional card.

---

### TEAM-UP

The team-up keyword names **two characters**.

- To **include** in a deck: the player's chosen identity must match one of the named characters.
- To **play**: both named friendly characters (identity or ally) must be **in play**.
- An ally counts as a named character if either its **title or subtitle** matches the named character.

---

### TEAMWORK (TRAIT)

After a minion with **teamwork** enters play and engages a player, if there is at least one other minion that shares the specified trait in play, **the minion that just entered play activates against the player it is engaged with**.

---

### TEMPORARY

A card with **temporary** must be **discarded from play at the end of the round**.

---

### TOUGHNESS

When a character with the **toughness** keyword **enters play**, place a **tough status card** on it.

---

### USES (X "TYPE")

When a card with this keyword **enters play**, place X all-purpose counters from the token pool on the card. The word following the value establishes the **type** of uses (e.g., "charge counters").

- Each card with uses also has an ability referencing that type of use as part of the cost.
- When the **last all-purpose counter is removed** from a card with uses (and the effect resolves): **discard that card**.
- All-purpose counters take on the type defined by the card they are on. When moved to a new card, they lose the previous type and gain the type defined by the new card.

---

### VICTORY X

When a card with the **victory X** keyword leaves play under the following conditions, place it in the **victory display** instead of the discard pile:

- A **character or side scheme** → placed in the victory display when **defeated**.
- An **attachment or upgrade** → placed in the victory display when the card **it is attached to** is defeated. (The attached card is discarded as normal.)
- A card with both **victory X and uses** → placed in the victory display when its **last all-purpose counter is removed**.

While in the victory display, X = the number of **victory points** that card is worth.

---

### VILLAINOUS

When a minion with the **villainous** keyword **activates**, give it a **facedown boost card** from the top of the encounter deck.

- When resolving that minion's activation: turn the boost card faceup, resolve any boost ability, and apply its boost icons to the minion's stats for this activation.
- Discard the boost card after it is resolved.
- If a boost ability on a boost card dealt to a minion refers to "the villain," that ability **still applies to the villain** (even though a minion is resolving it).

---

## STATUS CARD KEYWORDS (Stalwart, Steady, Tough, Toughness, Vulnerable)

These keywords interact directly with status cards. See also: glossary-P-Z.md (Status Cards).

### TOUGH (Status)

- If a character with a **tough status card** would take **any amount of damage**: prevent all of that damage and **discard one tough status card** instead.
  - A character with multiple tough cards discards **only one** each time it would take damage.
  - A hero with tough making a **basic defense**: reduce damage by DEF first. If reduced to 0, the hero does **not** lose their tough status card.
- A character is **not considered to have taken damage** when a tough status card prevents damage.

### VULNERABLE

When a character with **vulnerable** becomes **confused or stunned**: that character is **immediately discarded**.

- If a vulnerable character would simultaneously take enough damage to defeat it **and** become confused or stunned: it is **discarded before the damage is applied** and is **not** considered defeated.
- If a character has both **steady** and **vulnerable**: vulnerable does not take effect until the character has **two** confused or two stunned status cards.
