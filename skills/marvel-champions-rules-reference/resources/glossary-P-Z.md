# Marvel Champions — Glossary P–Z

**Source:** Rules Reference v1.7 | **Referenced by:** SKILL.md

---

## OWNERSHIP AND CONTROL

- **Owner** = the player whose deck contained the card at the start of setup. The scenario owns the encounter deck and each encounter card.
- Identity cards are owned and controlled by the player playing as that identity.
- Cards enter play under their **owner's control**. Encounter cards are under the **scenario's control**.
  - Upgrades attached to a card controlled by a different player are controlled by **that other player**.
  - When a player takes control of a campaign-specific or scenario-specific player card (with a player card back): that player becomes the **owner** until the game ends or another player takes control.
- Control **remains constant** unless an ability explicitly causes it to change.
- A player controls cards in their **own out-of-play areas** (hand, deck, discard pile).
- If a character **changes control while in play**: remains in the same state; moves to the new controller's play area.
- Upgrades on a card that changes control **also change control** to the same new controller.

**A change in control remains in effect until:**

- The ability that changed control **ceases to be in effect**.
- That card **leaves play** → placed in its owner's equivalent out-of-play area (or removed from the game if that player is no longer in the game).
- That card is an **event that was played** → placed in owner's discard pile.
- That card is **discarded from a player's hand** → placed in owner's discard pile.

## PLAY / PUT INTO PLAY

**Playing a card:** Paying the card's cost and placing it in the play area (entering play), or for events, resolving its ability and placing it in the discard pile. Cards are played from a **player's hand**.

**Putting a card into play:** Caused by abilities. **Bypasses paying costs and any restrictions/prohibitions** on playing the card. Enters play in the controller's play area.

- A card that is put into play is **not** considered to have been played.
- When put into play: its **resource cost is ignored**.
- Unless otherwise stated, cards put into play must enter a **play area or state that matches** the rules of playing the card.
- Cards with "[type] form only" can only be played or put into play by a player in the **specified form**.

---

## PLAY AREA

Two types:

**Player's play area:** Contains identity card, deck, hand, discard pile, HP dial, cards in play under their control, and any faceup/facedown encounter cards in their area (e.g., engaged minions, dealt encounter cards).

**Villain's play area:** Contains the villain deck, main scheme deck, encounter deck, encounter discard pile, and any encounter cards in play not in a player's play area (e.g., side schemes, environments).

- A card **cannot be in more than one play area** at a time.
- Cards **cannot be played into another player's play area** unless a game rule or card ability states otherwise.

---

## PLAYER

A person playing in a game of Marvel Champions. Each player has their own play area. Each player owns the cards in their deck (including identity card) at the start of the game.

---

## PLAYER CARD

Seven player card types: **Ally, Event, Identity, Player Side Scheme, Resource, Support, Upgrade**. Most player cards have a **blue** card back.

---

## PLAYER DECK

Contains the player cards owned by that player at the start of the game. Order cannot be changed unless a game step, game function, or card ability instructs otherwise.

**If a player deck empties:**

- Shuffle discard pile into a new deck; deal yourself **1 facedown encounter card**.
- If emptied while **drawing**: continue drawing up to the specified number.
- If emptied while **discarding**: no further cards are discarded from the newly shuffled deck.
- If emptied with **no cards in discard pile**: deck does not reset until at least 1 card enters discard pile; then deal 1 facedown encounter card.

---

## PLAYER ELIMINATION

A player is eliminated if their **identity is defeated** (remaining HP reduced to 0).

**When a player is eliminated, perform in order:**

1. If the eliminated player has the **first player token**: pass it clockwise.
2. If **minions are engaged** with the eliminated player: each minion engages the next clockwise player, retaining all tokens, attached cards, boost cards, tucked cards, and status cards.
3. For each card in the **eliminated player's play area not owned by that player**:
   - Attachment with permanent keyword: resolve its "attach to" text, or remove from game if no valid target.
   - Non-attachment card with permanent keyword: remove from game.
   - All other cards: place in owner's discard pile.
4. Place each card **owned by the eliminated player** in the eliminated player's discard pile.
5. Remove the eliminated player's **play area and each other game element within it** from the game.

- Remaining players continue; eliminated players no longer participate but win or lose with the group.
- If **all players are eliminated**: players lose.
- If eliminated partway through resolving an ability: **resolve the entire ability**.
- Effects referring to players in the game **ignore eliminated players**, except for the per player icon (👤).

---

## PLAYER SIDE SCHEME

Player side schemes are missions heroes undertake. The player card equivalent of encounter deck side schemes.

- Placed **next to the main scheme in the villain's play area** when played.
- Enters play with threat equal to its **starting threat value**.
- Limited by the **player side scheme limit**: 1–2 players → limit 1; 3–4 players → limit 2. If ever more in play than the limit: first player chooses and discards down to the limit.
- Any rules/effects referring to "schemes" or "side schemes" also refer to player side schemes.

## PLAY RESTRICTIONS AND PERMISSIONS

- To use an ability or play a card: **all play restrictions must be observed**.
- A **permission** is an optional play restriction allowing a player to use an ability or play a card outside normal timing or specifications.

---

## PREVENT

**Preventing damage:** Reduces the amount of damage the target **takes** (not the amount dealt). If all damage is prevented: the character is **not** considered to have taken damage. If all damage from an attack is prevented: the attacker is considered to have dealt damage but **not** "attacked and damaged" the attacked character.

- Dealing damage as a cost: cost is paid **even if** some/all damage is prevented.
- Taking damage as a cost: cost is **not** paid unless all damage was taken (if any is prevented, cost is not paid).

**Preventing threat:** Reduces the amount of threat being assigned **before** it is placed on the scheme.

---

## PRINTED

The text, characteristic, or value **physically printed** on the card. If a player must pay a cost using "printed resources": wild resources (★) cannot be spent as other resource types for such a cost.

---

## QUALIFIERS

If ability text includes a qualifier followed by multiple terms, the qualifier applies to **each item in the list**.

---

## READY

Cards enter play in a **ready state**. If instructed to ready an exhausted card: return it to its ready state. If there is an **additional cost** to ready a card: the player can choose not to pay; if they do not pay, the card **does not ready**.

---

## RECOVER

Recover is a basic power usable in **alter-ego form**. Exhaust the alter-ego and heal HP equal to **REC value**. An identity with **no damage to heal** cannot perform a basic recovery.

---

## REFERENTIAL ABILITY

Some abilities refer to specific cards by name. If an ability refers to a title shared by multiple cards, it refers only to the card(s) matching the **highest criteria** in this list:

1. The card on which the referential ability is printed (**self-referential**).
2. Cards that belong to the **same identity**.
3. **Player cards** (if the ability is on a player card) or **encounter cards** (if the ability is on an encounter card).

---

## REPLACEMENT EFFECT

Replaces a specified effect with a different effect. Most are interrupt abilities in the format: "when [triggering condition] would happen, do [replacement effect] instead."

- When an effect is replaced: it is **no longer considered imminent** and **no further interrupts or responses** to that effect can be triggered.

See: keywords.md (Requirement).

## RESOURCE

Resources are used to pay costs.

- Generate resources by **discarding cards from hand** or using **"Resource" card abilities**.
- Four types: **energy (⚡), mental (🧠), physical (💪), wild (★)**. Wild can be used as any type.
- To pay a resource cost: generate resources **equal to or greater than** the cost. **Excess resources are lost.**
- For most cards: any type or mix of types. For ability costs requiring specific types: specified types and quantities must be generated.

**Resource Ability:** Triggered ability indicated by bold "**Resource**" timing trigger. Can be triggered anytime the controlling player is **generating resources** to pay a cost. A resource ability generating a resource only for a specific type of cost cannot be triggered when paying a cost not of that type.

**Resource Card:** A player card type primarily **discarded from hand** to generate resources. Some resource cards have text that is active **while using the card to generate resources**. Resource cards are considered an **extension of an identity**.

---

## RESPONSE

A triggered ability indicated by the bold "**Response**" timing trigger. Executed **after** the specified triggering condition occurs.

- Players can only trigger on **cards they control** or **encounter cards** (not obligations in other players' play areas).
- Each response can only be triggered **once per occurrence** of its triggering condition.
- Multiple copies of the same card can each trigger on the same condition.
- If a single effect causes **multiple triggering conditions** to occur: responses to each can be resolved in **any order**.
- Once all players decline to use further responses: that response window is **closed**.
- First player has **first opportunity** to use a response; then proceeds clockwise.
- Forced responses initiate **before** non-forced responses for the same condition.

## REVEAL

During step 4 of the villain phase, each player (in player order) reveals and resolves all facedown encounter cards dealt to them, **one card at a time in the order dealt**.

**To reveal an encounter card:**

1. Turn the encounter card faceup.
2. Based on card type:
   - **Attachment** → enters play attached to the specified game element.
   - **Environment** → enters play in the villain's play area.
   - **Minion** → enters play in the revealing player's play area; engages that player.
   - **Obligation** → enters play in the revealing player's play area (or specified player's play area).
   - **Side scheme** → enters play in the villain's play area.
   - **Treachery** → placed on the table in front of the revealing player (not in play).
   - **Other** → placed on the table in front of the revealing player (not in play).
3. Resolve each "**When Revealed**" ability on that card (including those from keywords).
4. If the card is a **treachery**: discard it.

## SCHEME (CARD TYPE)

The word "scheme" denotes three card types: **main schemes, player side schemes, and side schemes**. If a card ability places threat on or removes threat from "a scheme": the resolving player **chooses** which scheme that ability affects.

---

## SCHEME (ENEMY ACTIVATION)

A scheme is a type of enemy activation.

**Steps to resolve:**

1. Give villain or villainous minion one facedown boost card. (Skip for non-villainous minions.)
2. Resolve each boost card (one at a time, in order dealt): Flip faceup → resolve any "Boost" ability → increase SCH by boost icons → discard. Repeat for remaining boost cards.
3. Place threat on the main scheme equal to the scheming enemy's **modified SCH value**.

---

## SEARCH

When instructed to search for a card: look at each card in the searched area; if you find a card satisfying the criteria, add it to the game area indicated by the search effect. If multiple satisfy the criteria: the player **chooses** among them. If **any portion of a deck** is searched: upon completion, **shuffle that entire deck**. If instructed to "search [their] collection": look through all Marvel Champions cards outside the current game; they become the **owner** of that card until the end of the game.

---

## SIDE SCHEME

An encounter card type representing additional obstacles and distractions.

- When revealed: enters play in the **villain's play area**, next to the main scheme deck.
- Enters play with threat equal to its **starting threat value**.
- Remains in play until: no threat on it (defeated and discarded), or a card ability removes it.
- Threat can be removed via heroes/allies using thwart power or card abilities.

---

## STATUS CARDS

Represent different states a character may find themselves in.

- A character **cannot have more than one status card of each type** at a time.
  - Characters with the **steady** keyword can have one additional confused and one additional stunned status card.
- Status card abilities have **timing priority** over all conflicting triggered abilities.

| Status       | Effect                                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Confused** | Cancels a character's next scheme or thwart. Discard the confused card instead; costs still paid. See glossary-A-D.md (Confuse). |
| **Stunned**  | Cancels a character's next attack. Discard the stunned card instead; costs still paid. See below (Stun).                         |
| **Tough**    | Prevents a character from taking damage. See below (Tough).                                                                      |

---

## STUN / STUNNED

Stun is a **status** that cancels a character's next attack.

- **Steady** keyword: stunned only if it has **two** stunned status cards.
- A character that "cannot be stunned": cannot have stunned status cards placed on it.
- If a **stunned identity or ally** attempts to attack or use an attack ability:
  - Discard the stunned card instead; costs (including exhausting) **must still be paid**.
  - A stunned character **can** attempt to attack even with no valid target.
- If a **stunned villain or minion** would attack: discard the stunned status card instead.
- The attack was replaced by removal of the stunned card; the character is **not** considered to have attacked.

---

## SUPPORT

A player card type representing locations, backline supporters, and other behind-the-scenes elements.

- Enters play in the **back row** of a player's play area.
- Active while in play; remains until a card ability causes it to leave play.
- Attacks, thwarts, defenses, action abilities, and triggered abilities from supports are **not** considered performed by the player's identity.

See: keywords.md (Surge).

## "SWAP"

Means to **exchange the location** of two components. Cannot be completed if there is not a component in both locations.

- Swapping a card in hand with the top card of a deck is **not** considered drawing that card.
- **Same title**: neither card enters or leaves play; tokens, attached cards, tucked cards, and status cards transfer; the other card maintains the state (ready or exhausted) of the previously in-play card; HP dial stays at the same value.
- **Different title**: in-play card leaves play; out-of-play card enters play ready; tokens, attached cards, tucked cards, and status cards are **not** transferred; HP dial is **reset** to the new card's printed HP value.

---

## TARGET

If a game function or card ability is directed toward a game element, that element becomes the **target** for the duration of that function's or ability's resolution.

**A target is valid if any part of the ability can affect that target.** A target is **not valid** if:

- The ability requires one or more targets and has **none valid**.
- The ability would cause the target to perform a game function that another ability says the target **cannot perform**.
- The ability's only effect on that target is to deal it damage and the target "**cannot take damage**."
- The target **cannot be attacked** and it's an attack-labeled ability.
- The target **cannot be thwarted** and it's a thwart-labeled ability.

**Additional rules:**

- The **cost** of an ability is **not considered** when determining if it can affect a target.
- Damage that is dealt but not taken (e.g., prevented) **is considered** to affect a target.
- "That [target]" refers to the target of a previous effect; can only be resolved for a valid target of the previous effect.
- An ability targeting multiple game elements (e.g., "each enemy") can be initiated if **at least one** is a valid target; it does not resolve against invalid targets.
- An ability referring to a **future target** (e.g., "the next card you play") does **not** require a target to initiate.
- An ability with a **search effect** requires only a searchable game area.
- A character with ATK, SCH, or THW of 0 **can** perform a basic power using that value against an otherwise valid target.

## TEXT BOX

The area of a card containing printed abilities, traits, and flavor text. If a card ability references a card's "text box": only references the **printed abilities** within that text box. Icons printed within a card's text box are considered **abilities** within that text box.

---

## "THEN"

The text preceding "then" must be **fully true or resolved** before the text after "then" can be resolved.

- Pre-"then" text fully resolves → post-"then" text **must** also attempt to resolve.
- Pre-"then" text does **not** fully resolve → post-"then" text does **not** attempt to resolve.

---

## THREAT

Threat tokens track the amount of threat on scheme cards.

---

## THWART

Ways a thwart attempt can occur:

1. **Basic thwart**: Hero or ally exhausts and uses THW power; removes threat equal to THW from the scheme. Can only initiate a basic thwart if there is a scheme with at least one threat **or** if the character is confused.
2. **Triggered ability labeled (thwart)**: Resolving it counts as a thwart. Hero does **not** exhaust (unless specified). A single labeled thwart is one thwart even if it removes multiple instances of threat.

If an ability increases threat removed by a thwart-labeled ability and it removes multiple instances: **each instance not using the word "additional"** is increased by the specified amount.

---

## TOUGH

Tough is a **status** that prevents a character from taking damage.

- If a character with a **tough status card** would take **any amount of damage**: prevent all of that damage and **discard one tough status card** instead.
  - Multiple tough cards: discard **only one** each time it would take damage.
  - Hero with tough making a **basic defense**: reduce damage by DEF first. If reduced to 0, the hero does **not** lose their tough status card.
  - Hero with tough takes **DEF-reduced damage** — if the remaining damage > 0, the tough status card is discarded, and the damage is prevented; if remaining damage = 0, tough status card is kept.
- A character is **not considered to have taken damage** when a tough status card prevents damage.
- When an ally or minion with a tough status card is dealt excess damage by an **overkill** attack: overkill damage is **not** dealt to the identity or villain.

**Toughness:** When a character with the toughness keyword **enters play**, place a **tough status card** on it.

---

## TRAITS

Listed at the top of the text box in **bold italics**. No inherent effects; some card abilities reference traits. Traits are **not** considered part of a card's printed text box for card ability purposes.

---

## TREACHERY

An encounter card type representing tactics, tricks, disasters, and immediate occurrences.

- The **revealing player** must resolve its effects.
- After resolving (or effects are canceled): placed in the **encounter discard pile**.
- If a treachery causes enemies to activate as its **last effect**: the treachery is considered resolved and discarded **after all of those activations have resolved**.

---

## TRIGGERED ABILITY

Indicated by a bold timing trigger followed by a colon and the rest of the ability text. Types: Action, Boost, Forced (Action/Interrupt/Response), Interrupt, Resource, Response, Setup, Special, When Completed, When Defeated, When Revealed.

---

## TRIGGERING CONDITION

A specific occurrence that takes place in the game. The triggering condition usually follows "**when**" or "**after**."

- Each "Interrupt" and "Response" can only be triggered **once per occurrence** of its triggering condition.
- Multiple copies of the same card can each be triggered by the same condition.
- If a single occurrence creates **multiple triggering conditions** simultaneously: those conditions are handled with a **single interrupt window** and a **single response window**. During each window, abilities referring to any of those triggering conditions may be used in **any order**.

---

## UNIQUE ICON (✦)

Indicates a card representing a singular person, place, or thing.

**Two unique cards "match" if any of the following apply:**

- They share a title, and both have no subtitle and no alter-ego title.
- The subtitle or alter-ego title of one matches the title, subtitle, or alter-ego title of the other.

**Rules:**

- A player **cannot include multiple matching cards** in their deck (identity card included in this evaluation).
- When choosing identities during setup: players **cannot choose matching identities**.
- Players **may** choose a scenario even if villains match chosen identities.
- A **non-villain card in an out-of-play state** that matches a card in play **cannot enter play**:
  - **Player card**: cannot be played or put into play; any effect attempting to do so has no effect.
  - **Non-villain encounter card**: discarded; any effects of it entering play or being revealed are ignored; the revealing player is dealt a facedown encounter card.

---

## VILLAIN / VILLAIN DECK

An encounter card type representing the **primary enemy**. A sequential deck of one or more cards; players defeat by reducing HP of each stage to zero. Villain cards **cannot be discarded from play**.

---

## VILLAIN DEFEAT

If the villain's HP dial reaches 0: players have **defeated that stage**.

**Process:**

1. Remove the current stage from the game.
2. Reveal the next sequential villain stage; set the HP dial as indicated.

**If the final stage is defeated: the players win the game.** The revealing of a villain **cannot be canceled**.

**Same title as previous stage:**

- Considered the **same character** for ability purposes.
- Attachments, upgrades, status cards, counters, and non-damage tokens **carry over**.
- If villain was defeated while activating: the villain **resumes that activation** using the new stage.

**Different title from previous stage:**

- **Not** considered the same character for ability purposes.
- Attachments, upgrades, status cards, counters, and non-damage tokens do **not** carry over.
- If villain was defeated while activating: the **activation ends** without resolving.

Excess damage dealt to defeat a villain stage does **not** carry over.

## WINNING THE GAME

- **Final villain stage defeated** → players **win**.
- **Final main scheme stage completed** → villain **wins**.
- **All players eliminated** → players **lose**.
- Some scenarios have alternate win or loss conditions; these **cannot be blanked or canceled**.

---

## "WOULD"

Used to define the triggering condition of some interrupt abilities, establishing a **higher timing priority** than interrupts to the same condition without "would."

- If an interrupt to a "would" triggering condition **changes the nature** of what is about to occur: **no further interrupts** to the original trigger may be used.
- _Example: "When a character would be defeated" triggers before "when a character is defeated."_

---

## YOU / YOUR

If "you" **can** refer to the player's identity, it **must**:

| Phrase                                   | Meaning                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Deal damage to you" / "you take damage" | Applied to the HP dial of the **player's identity**         |
| "Deal indirect damage to you"            | Player distributes among characters under their control     |
| "You exhaust"                            | The **player's identity** exhausts                          |
| "You get a status card"                  | Placed on the **player's identity**                         |
| "After you attack/defeat/thwart"         | The **identity** they control performed it (not allies)     |
| "After [enemy] attacks you"              | The **attacked player**, even if they defended with an ally |

If "you" **cannot** refer to the identity, it refers to the **player** (e.g., "discard cards from your hand," "search your deck," game functions against non-identity cards).

**Cards considered extensions of a player's identity:** Events, Resources, Upgrades (unless attached to a different friendly character).

**Cards NOT considered extensions:** Allies, Encounter cards, Player Side Schemes, Supports.
