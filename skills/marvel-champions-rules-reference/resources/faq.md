# Marvel Champions — FAQ

**Source:** Rules Reference v1.7 | **Referenced by:** SKILL.md

> Official Q&A for specific cards and scenarios. When a FAQ entry conflicts with a general rule, the FAQ takes precedence for that specific card. Always check `errata.md` first for corrected card text before consulting this file.

## GENERAL QUESTIONS

**Q: If my hero has a tough status card and I am dealt damage, can I use an interrupt ability to reduce or prevent that damage in order to keep the tough status card?**

A: No. Status cards have priority over all triggered abilities (including interrupts), so the tough status card must be discarded to prevent all of the damage before any other abilities could trigger.

A hero _can_ keep their tough status card if:

1. A **constant effect** reduces the damage the hero takes to zero (constant effects have priority over status cards).
2. The hero makes a **basic defense** and their DEF reduces the damage dealt by the attacker's ATK to zero (a basic defense reduces the amount of damage _dealt_ by the attacker, not the amount of damage _taken_ by the defender).

## CORE SET

**Spider-Man (#1A) — When does the villain "initiate" an attack against Spider-Man?**
An attack is "initiated" the moment the game determines an attack will be made against a character. This includes attacks from game steps (step 2 of the villain phase) and card abilities. Spider-Man's ability resolves before any steps of the enemy attack process are performed.

**Webbed Up (#9) — How many attacks does Webbed Up prevent?**
Two attacks total. Webbed Up prevents the attached enemy's very next attack (replacing it with placement of a stun status card), then the stun status card prevents that enemy's following attack.

**Webbed Up (#9) — Does Webbed Up prevent Spider-Sense from triggering?**
Yes. Webbed Up's ability is a replacement effect (indicated by "instead"), meaning the attack never initiates.

**Jennifer Walters (#19B) — Can Jennifer Walters remove threat from a side scheme as it enters play?**
No. Side schemes enter play with threat already on them, meaning threat is not "placed" at this point.

**Jennifer Walters (#19B) — If threat is placed on a scheme during game setup, does Jennifer Walters's ability trigger?**
No. Abilities without the "Setup" timing trigger cannot resolve during game setup.

**Legal Practice (#23) — Can Legal Practice remove threat from multiple schemes simultaneously?**
No. All of the threat removed by Legal Practice must be removed from a single scheme.

**Focused Rage (#27) — If She-Hulk has a tough status card, can she use Focused Rage to draw a card?**
No. The tough status card prevents She-Hulk from "tak[ing] 1 damage," so the ability's cost cannot be paid. You cannot attempt to pay the cost just to remove She-Hulk's tough status card.

**Superhuman Strength (#28) — If She-Hulk attacks an enemy that cannot be stunned (already stunned or stalwart), does Superhuman Strength's Forced Interrupt resolve?**
No. Because the effect cannot affect the attacked enemy, it cannot resolve and Superhuman Strength is not discarded.

**Repulsor Blast (#31) — Can Repulsor Blast's first point of damage remove a tough status card, then its additional damage deal damage to that same enemy?**
No. The additional damage is a simultaneous modification of the first point. All damage is dealt at once.

**Pepper Potts (#33) — Does Pepper Potts generate 2 resources if a card with 2 printed resources (such as Energy, Genius, or Strength) is on top of the discard pile?**
Yes. Pepper Potts generates a number of resources equal in quantity and type to the resources on the top card of the discard pile.

**Pepper Potts (#33) — Will Pepper Potts generate 2 resources if a "The Power of [Aspect]" resource card is on top of the discard pile?**
No. Pepper Potts is the card generating the resources. "The Power of [Aspect]" never generates resources itself, having no opportunity to generate additional resources for a card of its specified aspect.

**Pepper Potts (#33) — Can Pepper Potts generate the resources of a card that is currently being spent?**
No. Resources are generated simultaneously, meaning the spent card will not be on top of the discard pile when Pepper Potts uses her ability.

**Rocket Boots (#39) — If Iron Man has 1 hit point remaining and his Rocket Boots upgrade is discarded from play, is Iron Man defeated?**
Yes. If an ability that modifies hit points expires, the modified hit points revert to the value they would be without the modifier. In this case, Iron Man's hit points would revert to 0, instantly defeating him.

**Black Panther (#40A) — If an enemy initiates an attack against Black Panther but another hero or an ally defends, will Black Panther's retaliate ability trigger?**
No. Black Panther himself must be attacked for retaliate to trigger.

**Black Panther (#40A) — If Black Panther defends against an attack, will his retaliate ability trigger?**
Yes. As long as Black Panther himself is attacked, retaliate will trigger.

**Ancestral Knowledge (#42) — Can Ancestral Knowledge shuffle different versions of Wakanda Forever into Black Panther's deck?**
No. Cards with the same title are considered to be the same card for the purpose of card abilities.

**Vibranium Suit (#49) — Does moving damage discard tough status cards from the target enemy?**
Yes. If damage is moved to a character, the moved damage is considered to be dealt to that character.

**Tigra (#51) — Does Tigra's "Response" ability trigger before or after she takes consequential damage?**
Before. The "After Tigra attacks and defeats a minion..." condition is checked immediately after Tigra defeats a minion with her attack power, but before consequential damage is applied.

**Make the Call (#71) — Do "The Power of [Aspect]" resource cards generate 1 or 2 resources when playing Make the Call?**
If the ally's aspect matches the aspect of The Power of [Aspect] card, it will generate 2 resources. If the ally's aspect does not match, The Power of [Aspect] card will only generate 1 resource.

**Whirlwind (#130) — How is Whirlwind's attack against each hero resolved?**
Whirlwind makes a separate attack against each hero and these attacks are resolved simultaneously. Each attack may have a defender declared.

**Ultron II (#135) — If Ultron's attack is defended by a different player, does Ultron get +1 ATK for Drone minions engaged with the defending player or the originally attacked player?**
The bonus is based on Drone minions engaged with the player Ultron **initiated the attack against**. Ultron's ability creates a lasting effect referring to the original target.

**Assault (#197) — If Assault causes the villain to attack me and there is only one card left in the encounter deck, is Assault in the encounter discard pile when the discard pile is shuffled?**
No. Assault is not discarded until it finishes resolving, which happens when the villain's attack has been resolved.

## GREEN GOBLIN SCENARIO PACK

**Norman Osborn (#1A) — Will a stun status card prevent Norman Osborn's attack activation?**
Yes. Status cards take priority over all other abilities, so a stun status card will prevent Norman Osborn's activation.

**Green Goblin (#1B) — While Green Goblin is attacking, if the last madness counter is removed through a "Boost" ability, what happens?**
Green Goblin immediately flips to Norman Osborn form. Boost icons are added to Norman Osborn's ATK value of ★ (treated as 0). Norman Osborn's "Forced Interrupt" does not trigger in this specific situation, as the attack is already past the point of "When Norman Osborn would attack..." by the time he changes form.

**I See You (#30) — If I'm in alter-ego form and I reveal "I See You", does Green Goblin attack me?**
Yes. "I See You" does not specify hero form or alter-ego form, so Green Goblin will attack regardless.

## CAPTAIN AMERICA HERO PACK

**Steve Rogers (#1B) — If Captain America's Shield is put into play as a Drone minion during setup for Ultron, can I retrieve it with Steve Rogers's Setup ability?**
No. The Setup ability specifically searches the player's deck and discard pile only.

**Steve Rogers (#1B) — If I change form to Steve Rogers after an ally is played while I'm Captain America, will "Living Legend" still apply to the next ally played?**
No. Living Legend only applies to the very first ally played each round.

## MS. MARVEL HERO PACK

**Embiggen (#10) — If an Attack event deals multiple sources of damage (such as Melee), how does Embiggen work?**
Embiggen increases all instances of damage that an Attack event deals by 2. If an event deals 3 damage then 3 damage again, it would now deal 5 damage, then 5 damage again.

**Shrink (#11) — When playing Emergency, can I exhaust Shrink to remove 2 threat?**
No. Shrink increases each instance of threat removed by a thwart event. Emergency only prevents threat and does not remove any.

**Nova/Sam Alexander (#12) — If Nova's ability defeats an attacking enemy, does that enemy's attack still deal damage?**
No. If Nova defeats the enemy during the attack sequence before damage is calculated, the rest of the attack sequence fails to resolve.

**Melee (#30) — Can Melee deal damage to the same enemy twice?**
No. When Melee deals damage a second time, that damage must be dealt to a different enemy. Note that different stages of the villain are considered to be the same enemy.

**Melee (#30) — If I'm engaged with a minion with guard, can I target that minion and the villain, as long as the minion would be destroyed by Melee's first sentence?**
Yes, as long as you defeat the minion with the first sentence of Melee, you may deal damage to the villain with the second sentence.

## THOR HERO PACK

**Jarnbjorn (#19) — Can I trigger Jarnbjorn after I play an Attack event?**
Yes. A hero is considered to make an attack both through basic attack power and actions with the (attack) label.

**Jarnbjorn (#19) — If an Attack event damages multiple enemies, can Jarnbjorn be triggered for each one?**
Yes. Each enemy that is dealt damage by an Attack event is considered to have been attacked.

## BLACK WIDOW HERO PACK

**Dance of Death (#4) — Dance of Death does not contain the (attack) label. Are its damage-dealing effects actually considered attacks?**
Yes. The first sentence of Dance of Death's ability defines each damage-dealing effect as an individual attack.

**Dance of Death (#4) — If Black Widow is stunned, will each attack from Dance of Death be prevented, or just the first?**
Because Dance of Death creates three separate attacks, a stun status card will only prevent the first attack. The second and third can be performed as normal.

**Attacrobatics (#6) — Can Attacrobatics cancel 0 boost icons?**
No. Attacrobatics cannot be used to cancel boost icons on a card that has none.

**Widow's Bite (#10) — If I have Widow's Bite in play and I reveal a minion with quickstrike, which resolves first?**
Quickstrike resolves first. Keywords have timing priority over "Response" abilities.

## DOCTOR STRANGE HERO PACK

**The Night Nurse (#19) — If the only status card on a hero is a tough status card, is it discarded if I use The Night Nurse to heal that hero?**
Yes. The Night Nurse's ability discards 1 status card from that hero, no matter the type.

**Unflappable (#20) — If while defending my identity takes damage from a "Boost" ability but takes no damage from the attacking enemy otherwise, can I still trigger Unflappable?**
Yes. The cost only requires that the defending identity take no damage during step 4 of the enemy attack.

**Counterspell (#30) — If I draw Counterspell while in alter-ego form, what happens?**
Counterspell attempts to attach to your hero but cannot. Because it is unable to meet its condition, simply discard it. Do not reveal a new encounter card in its place.

**Images of Ikonn (#33) — Can I resolve Images of Ikonn if the villain is already confused and there is no threat on a scheme?**
Yes. As long as any part of the ability has a valid target, it will do as many effects as it is able. Its second effect always has a valid target (the card itself).

## HULK HERO PACK

**Crushing Blow (#2) — If Crushing Blow's resource cost is reduced to 0, can it be played without spending 💪 resources?**
Yes. The "You can only spend 💪 resources" ability only applies when actual resources are being spent. If the cost is reduced to 0, no resources are required.

**Unstoppable Force (#6) — If Unstoppable Force's resource cost is reduced to 0, will I draw 1 card from its second ability?**
No. Because no resources were spent, its "If you paid for this ability using only 💪 resources..." condition was not fulfilled.

**Clash of Titans (#28) — If my highest-ATK character is an ally and the attack from Clash of Titans is undefended, which takes the damage: my identity or the ally?**
The ally will take all damage from the undefended attack.

## RISE OF RED SKULL EXPANSION

**Hawkeye's Quiver (#3) — After using Hawkeye's Quiver to search the top 5 cards of my deck, what should I do with those cards?**
Shuffle those cards back into Hawkeye's deck. After any search ability is completed (whether it searched the whole deck or only a portion), the entire deck is shuffled.

**Marked for Death (#28) — When Marked for Death is revealed, if a copy of Mockingbird is already under a player's control who is not Clint Barton, what happens?**
The Clint Barton player must search for Mockingbird in their hand, deck, discard pile, and play area, though cannot place their Mockingbird under Marked for Death as there is already a unique character in play with the same title/subtitle. However, when Marked for Death is defeated, any copy of Mockingbird in play (even one controlled by another player) is returned to its owner's hand.

**Jessica Drew (#31B) — Does Jessica Drew's Double Agent ability require her deck to be built with two aspects?**
Yes. An equal number of cards from two different aspects must be included in her deck.

**Finesse (#33) — Can Finesse generate a resource to pay for an aspect card's ability?**
Yes. As long as the resource generated by Finesse is spent on an aspect card (its resource cost or a cost within that aspect card's ability), Finesse can be used.

## THE ONCE AND FUTURE KANG SCENARIO PACK

**While players are split into separate game areas, whose game area are environment cards considered to be in?**
Environment cards are considered to be in all players' game areas.

**Depowered (#20) — If Depowered is in Doctor Strange's play area, can the Doctor Strange player still use the Invocation deck?**
Yes. The abilities on cards in the Invocation deck are merely resolved, not played.

## WASP HERO PACK

**Wasp (#1C) — Giant form, first ability: How does it interact with the patrol keyword and the crisis icon?**
Wasp cannot choose the main scheme as a target while engaged with a patrol minion or while a crisis icon is in play. Due to the simultaneous nature of this ability, this applies even if the patrol minion or crisis icon card is removed from play during her basic thwart's resolution.

**Wasp (#1C) — Giant form, second ability: How does it interact with the guard keyword?**
Wasp cannot choose the villain as a target while engaged with a guard minion. Due to the simultaneous nature of this ability, this applies even if the guard minion is removed during her basic attack's resolution.

**Wasp (#1C) — Giant form, second ability: How does it interact with multiple enemies who have retaliate?**
Wasp is considered to attack each target affected by her divided basic attack. Each instance of retaliate will damage Wasp in the order of her choice.

## QUICKSILVER HERO PACK

**Quicksilver (#1A) — If Quicksilver attempts a basic attack or thwart while stunned or confused, can his Super Speed ability trigger?**
No. Stunned and confused status cards replace the usage of the basic power with their removal. Quicksilver is not considered to have used a basic power and the condition of Super Speed is not met.

## SCARLET WITCH HERO PACK

**Hex Bolt (#4) — Do the bulleted abilities resolve each time a card is discarded, or after all 3 cards have been discarded?**
Resolve the first sentence of Hex Bolt entirely, without interruption. Then determine and resolve the appropriate bulleted abilities based on what was discarded.

**Slipping Sanity (#23) — Is it intentional that Scarlet Witch has two obligation cards?**
Yes. Having multiple obligations represents the negative facets of her powers.

## GALAXY'S MOST WANTED EXPANSION

**Which sets are considered modular encounter sets?**
If an encounter set is not scenario-specific or campaign-specific, then it is modular. The eight modular encounter sets are: Badoon Headhunter, Band of Badoon, Galactic Artifacts, Kree Militants, Menagerie Medley, Power Stone, Space Pirates, and Ship Command.

**Campaign Mode — Do I have to play in expert mode for each scenario in an expert campaign?**
No. Increasing a campaign's difficulty merely adds additional cards or setup instructions. Players choose other modes of play for each individual scenario.

**Rocket Raccoon (#29A) — If his "Tinkering" ability is used but the Tech upgrade is not placed into a discard pile, will Rocket Raccoon draw 2 cards?**
Yes. Discarding is the act of attempting to place a card into a discard pile. Even if the Tech upgrade ends up elsewhere, the cost was still paid.

**Battery Pack (#34) — Can Battery Pack move a charge counter to a Tech card that does not use "charge counters"?**
Yes. The card that an all-purpose counter is on defines the type of that counter. Battery Pack can move a counter to any Tech card.

**Power Stone (#149) — If an identity who controls the Power Stone is defeated, what happens to the Power Stone?**
A permanent card owned by the scenario that was in an eliminated player's play area goes to the encounter discard pile.

**Power Stone (#149) — If a character with the Power Stone is dealt 3 or more damage but does not take 3 or more damage due to damage prevention, does the attacker get the Power Stone?**
Yes. Damage prevention does not reduce the amount of damage dealt. Note that a hero making a basic defense reduces the amount of damage dealt (not just taken), so defending can prevent the Power Stone from being taken.

## STAR-LORD HERO PACK

**Star-Lord (#1A) — If I play an ally without the printed Guardian trait while in hero form, can I trigger the response on Knowhere (#22)?**
Yes. Star-Lord's constant ability grants each ally he controls the Guardian trait. Constant abilities have priority over triggered abilities, so the ally will have the Guardian trait before Knowhere triggers.

**Dive Bomb (#28) — Can I play Dive Bomb if I am engaged with a minion with the guard keyword?**
Yes. The first sentence resolves first. If that damage defeats the guard minion, the second sentence can deal 1 damage to each other enemy including the villain. If the guard minion was not defeated, the second sentence deals 1 damage to each enemy other than the villain and the enemy dealt damage by the first sentence.

## MAD TITAN'S SHADOW EXPANSION

**Scenario #2 — Tower Defense: When a minion schemes, which main scheme is its threat placed on?**
Threat from a minion scheming is placed on the main scheme with the "Focused Defense" attachment attached to it.

**Scenario #4 — The Sinister Six: If there is more than one villain in play, which villain takes the excess damage from overkill attacks?**
The villain with the active counter takes the overkill damage.

**Scenario #4 — The Sinister Six: What happens if a villain needs to activate and no villain has the active counter?**
Place the active counter on the villain with the lowest activation order value and continue that activation.

**Scenario #5 — Venom Goblin: What happens when one of the main schemes is completed?**
When any of the main schemes has threat on it equal to or greater than its target threat value, flip that main scheme to its environment side and reveal that environment.

## SINISTER MOTIVES EXPANSION

**Across the Spider-Verse (#18) — Can this ability be repeated more than once?**
Yes. Repeating the ability includes the repeat effect itself. As long as a player who can spend 3 resources and exhaust a Web-Warrior card can be chosen, the ability can be repeated.

## IRONHEART HERO PACK

**"Go for Champions!" (#25) — If I have played "Go for Champions!" this round, can I trigger an ability that requires taking damage as a cost?**
No. If you cannot take damage, you cannot pay a cost that requires you to take damage.

## MUTANT GENESIS EXPANSION

**Powerful Punch (#14) — If Shadowcat plays Powerful Punch on the villain, when does she flip her mass form?**
When the villain initiates an attack, Shadowcat plays Powerful Punch and deals 4 damage to the villain. As soon as she finishes resolving the attack-labeled effect, she is considered to have attacked and can flip her mass form. The villain then continues its attack, and Shadowcat is now considered defending.

**White Queen (#56) — How does White Queen's constant ability work?**
White Queen continuously places confused status cards on the engaged player's identity until that identity cannot have any more. If that identity attempts to thwart, they remove their confused status card(s) but will immediately be given more. When White Queen leaves play, any confused status cards remain until that identity attempts to thwart to remove them.

**Fabian Cortez (#159) — If his "When Defeated" ability puts an Acolyte minion with teamwork into play while he is the only other Acolyte in play, will teamwork trigger?**
No. Since putting the other minion into play is the last effect of Fabian Cortez's "When Defeated" ability, he is discarded immediately upon the other minion entering play. Fabian Cortez is no longer in play when teamwork tries to find an Acolyte minion.

## AGE OF APOCALYPSE EXPANSION

**Magik (#30A) — When Magik plays the top card of her deck, at what point is the next card turned faceup?**
When playing the top card (the same process as playing from hand), Magik moves the card to the table during step 3 of the Initiating Abilities process. As soon as she does this, she turns the new top card of her deck faceup.

**Magik (#30A) — Can Magik play the top card of her deck when an ability says "play a card from your hand"?**
Yes. Any time Magik has an opportunity to play a card from her hand, she may choose to play the top card of her deck instead (once per phase).

**Magik (#30A) — Can Magik resolve an ability triggering "after you play [card] from your hand," if she plays that card from the top of her deck?**
Yes. When Magik plays a card from the top of her deck, that card is considered to have been played from her hand.

**Magik (#30A) — Can Magik put the top card of her deck into play as if it was in her hand?**
No. Putting a card into play is not the same as playing a card. Magik's ability only allows her to _play_ the top card of her deck.

## AGENTS OF S.H.I.E.L.D. EXPANSION

**Maria Hill (#1B) — Can I include just one or two S.H.I.E.L.D. supports from aspects other than my chosen aspect?**
No. Maria Hill's ability is all or nothing. You must include the maximum number of copies of each of exactly three S.H.I.E.L.D. supports from other aspects, or you cannot have any S.H.I.E.L.D. supports from other aspects in your deck.

## NEXT EVOLUTION EXPANSION

**Malice (#199) — Is Malice still a minion while she is attached to an ally?**
Yes. She retains the minion card type; retains any damage on her; can be attacked and targeted by card abilities; cannot be defeated again; is not considered engaged with a player and cannot activate; is discarded when the card she is attached to leaves play.

## X-23 HERO PACK

**Honey Badger (#3) — Can Honey Badger's "Response" be triggered if the damage defeats her?**
No. By the time Honey Badger's "Response" would trigger, she has already left play.
