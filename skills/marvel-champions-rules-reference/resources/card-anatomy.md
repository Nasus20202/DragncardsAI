## PLAYER CARD FIELDS

| #   | Field                           | Location                         | Description                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Title**                       | Top of card                      | The name of this card                                                                                                                    |
| 2   | **Card Type**                   | Below title                      | Indicates how this card behaves or may be used in the game                                                                               |
| 3   | **Traits**                      | Top of text box, bold italics    | Flavorful attributes that may be referenced by card abilities; **not** considered part of the printed text box for card ability purposes |
| 4   | **Ability**                     | Text box                         | This card's specialized means of interacting with the game                                                                               |
| 5   | **Cost**                        | Top-left corner                  | The resource cost to play this card                                                                                                      |
| 6   | **Hit Points**                  | Right side                       | A value representing this card's durability                                                                                              |
| 7   | **Resources**                   | Bottom-left corner               | The resources this card generates when discarded from hand                                                                               |
| 8   | **Deckbuilding Classification** | Bottom of card                   | Indicates if this card is exclusive to a hero, belongs to an aspect, or is a basic card                                                  |
| 9   | **THW**                         | Left side stat block             | How effective this character is at opposing enemy schemes                                                                                |
| 10  | **ATK**                         | Left side stat block             | How effective this character is when it attacks                                                                                          |
| 11  | **DEF**                         | Left side stat block             | How effective this character is when it defends                                                                                          |
| 12  | **REC**                         | Left side stat block (alter-ego) | How effective this alter-ego is at recovering damage                                                                                     |
| 13  | **Consequential Damage (⬤)**    | Beneath ATK or THW               | The amount of damage this ally takes after it is used for this power                                                                     |
| 14  | **Hand Size**                   | Bottom of card                   | The number of cards this card's controller resets their hand to each round                                                               |
| 15  | **Collector Information**       | Bottom of card                   | This card's product of origin (set icon) and card number within that product                                                             |
| 16  | **Unique Icon (✦)**             | In the title                     | Indicates that the card is unique                                                                                                        |
| 17  | **Subtitle**                    | Beneath the title                | Indicates an alternate identity an ally might possess                                                                                    |

## CARD BACKS

| Back Style         | Card Type      | Notes                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blue (standard)    | Player card    | Standard back for all player cards                                                                                        |
| Blue (alternate)   | Player card    | Used for cards that are never shuffled into the player deck (e.g., identity cards, permanent cards) — aids identification |
| Orange (standard)  | Encounter card | Standard back for all encounter cards                                                                                     |
| Orange (alternate) | Encounter card | Used for cards that are never shuffled into the encounter deck — aids identification                                      |
| Purple             | Villain card   | Used exclusively for villain cards                                                                                        |

## CARD TYPE QUICK REFERENCE

### Player Card Types

| Type                   | Card Back        | Key Rules                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Identity**           | Blue (alternate) | Double-sided (hero/alter-ego). Cannot be discarded from play. Faceup side is in play; facedown side is out of play.                    |
| **Ally**               | Blue             | Friendly character. Remains in play until defeated or discarded. Max 3 per player.                                                     |
| **Event**              | Blue             | Played for instantaneous effect. Not in play while being played. Placed in discard pile after effects resolve.                         |
| **Support**            | Blue             | Locations and backline helpers. Active while in play. Abilities are not considered performed by the player's identity.                 |
| **Upgrade**            | Blue             | Powers and equipment. Active while in play. Considered an extension of the identity unless attached to a different friendly character. |
| **Resource**           | Blue             | Primarily discarded to generate resources. Considered an extension of the identity when spent.                                         |
| **Player Side Scheme** | Blue             | Missions. Placed in villain's play area when played. Affected by thwart abilities.                                                     |

### Encounter Card Types

| Type            | Card Back          | Key Rules                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Villain**     | Purple             | Primary enemy. Sequential deck of stages. Cannot be discarded from play.                                           |
| **Main Scheme** | Orange (alternate) | Villain's primary objective. Advances when threat ≥ target threat. Final stage completed = villain wins.           |
| **Minion**      | Orange             | Supporters of the villain. Engage players and activate against them during step 2 of the villain phase.            |
| **Attachment**  | Orange             | Attaches to another card or game element when it enters play. Discarded when the attached element leaves play.     |
| **Environment** | Orange             | Creates overarching rules. Active while in play in the villain's play area. Considered in all players' game areas. |
| **Side Scheme** | Orange             | Additional obstacles. Placed in villain's play area when revealed. Defeated when it has no threat.                 |
| **Treachery**   | Orange             | Immediate effects. Placed on the table (not in play) when revealed; resolved and discarded.                        |
| **Obligation**  | Orange (alternate) | Commitments for an identity's alter-ego. Placed in that player's play area when revealed or drawn.                 |

## TEXT BOX RULES

- The text box contains **printed abilities, traits, and flavor text**.
- If a card ability references a card's "text box," it only references the **printed abilities** within that text box — not flavor text or traits.
- Icons printed within a card's text box are considered **abilities** within that text box.
- **Traits** (bold italics at the top of the text box) are **not** considered part of a card's printed text box for card ability purposes.
- The **divider line** within the text box of encounter cards separates regular ability text from the "Boost" ability text. Only the text **beneath the divider line** is active when a card is resolving as a boost card.

---

## BOOST FIELD

- Located at the **bottom-right** of encounter cards.
- **Boost icons (▼)**: each one increases the attacking enemy's ATK or scheming enemy's SCH by 1 during that activation.
- **Star icon (★)** in the boost field: indicates there is a mandatory "Boost" ability in the text box (beneath the divider line). The star icon itself is **not** a boost icon and does not contribute to ATK or SCH.
