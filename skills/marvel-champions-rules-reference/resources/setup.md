# Marvel Champions — Setup

**Source:** Rules Reference v1.7 | **Referenced by:** SKILL.md

---

## FULL SETUP SEQUENCE

To set up a game, perform the following steps **in order**:

---

### STEP 1 — Select Identities

Each player selects one identity, placing their **alter-ego side faceup**.

---

### STEP 2 — Set Hit Points

Each player sets their HP dial equal to the **starting hit points** of their character, found at the bottom of their identity card.

---

### STEP 3 — Select First Player

As a group, the players select a first player and place the **first player token** in front of that player.

---

### STEP 4 — Set Aside Obligations

For each identity being played, **set aside their obligation card(s)** out of play.

---

### STEP 5 — Set Aside Nemesis Sets

For each identity being played, **set aside their nemesis and the encounter cards of that nemesis** out of play.

---

### STEP 6 — Shuffle Player Decks

Each player **shuffles their player deck**.

---

### STEP 7 — Collect Tokens and Status Cards

Collect a pool of damage tokens, threat tokens, acceleration tokens, and all-purpose counters within reach of each player. Place stacks of stunned, confused, and tough status cards near this pool.

---

### STEP 8 — Select Scenario

Select a scenario and put its **villain deck and main scheme deck** into play near the center of the play area.

---

### STEP 9 — Set the Villain's Hit Points

Set the villain's HP dial to the value indicated by the **villain card**.

---

### STEP 10 — Create the Encounter Deck

Shuffle the encounter sets listed on **side 1A of the main scheme card** together with the **obligation cards** set aside during step 4 to create the encounter deck.

---

### STEP 11 — Put Setup Cards Into Play

Search each deck and the set-aside area for any cards with the **setup keyword** and put them into play.

---

### STEP 12 — Resolve Scenario Setup and When Revealed Abilities

- **a.** Resolve any "**Setup**" abilities on main scheme card 1A.
- **b.** Flip the main scheme card to **side 1B** and resolve any "**When Revealed**" abilities on that side.
- **c.** Resolve any "**Setup**" and "**When Revealed**" abilities on the villain.

---

### STEP 13 — Campaign Setup

If playing in campaign mode, resolve the Setup campaign instructions listed for the scenario in its associated rulebook.

---

### STEP 14 — Draw Cards

Each player draws cards from their deck until they have cards equal in number to their **hand size** (including modifiers), as listed near the bottom of their identity card.

---

### STEP 15 — Resolve Mulligans

Each player may discard any number of cards from their hand, then draw back up to their starting hand size. **Do not shuffle these discarded cards back into their decks at this time.**

---

### STEP 16 — Resolve Player Setup Abilities

Resolve any "**Setup**" abilities on player cards in play.

---

The game is now ready to begin.

---

## SETUP NOTES

| Rule                                 | Detail                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Player card "Setup" abilities        | Resolved at step 16 — **not** during steps 11–12                                                        |
| Encounter card "Setup" abilities     | Resolved at step 12a                                                                                    |
| Permanent keyword cards              | Set aside before step 1; put into play when referenced by other cards, not during step 11               |
| Linked keyword cards                 | Set aside at the start of the game if any deck includes the card that brings them into play             |
| Nemesis sets                         | Set aside at step 5; only brought into play by encounter card instructions during the game              |
| Obligations                          | Shuffled into the encounter deck at step 10                                                             |
| Player card abilities during setup   | Cannot resolve during game setup unless prefaced by a "Setup" timing trigger                            |
| When Revealed abilities during setup | If an encounter card with "When Revealed" enters play during setup, resolve that ability during step 12 |
| Identity selection                   | Players cannot choose identities that match (share a title or subtitle/alter-ego title)                 |
