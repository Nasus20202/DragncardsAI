# Marvel Champions — Glossary E–O

**Source:** Rules Reference v1.7 | **Referenced by:** SKILL.md

---

## DELAYED EFFECT

Some abilities contain delayed effects — specifying a future timing point or future condition and the effect to happen at that time.

- Resolve **automatically and immediately** after their specified timing point/condition occurs, before responses to that point may be used.
- Same **timing priority** as constant effects.
- When a delayed effect resolves, it is **not** treated as a new triggered ability.

---

## DISCARD

The act of attempting to move a card from a non-discard-pile play area to a discard pile.

- **Player card** discarded → placed faceup on top of the **owning player's** discard pile.
- **Encounter card** discarded → placed faceup on top of the **encounter discard pile**.
- Multiple cards discarded from a player's hand or from play by a singular effect: place in discard pile in **any order**.
- Multiple cards discarded from a **deck** by a singular effect: place one at a time **without changing the order**. These discards are considered **simultaneous**; responses are resolved after all discards are made.
- Cards looked at from top of a deck and then discarded: considered discarded from the **top of that deck**.

**Discard Pile:** An out-of-play area. Open information — any player may look at any time. Order cannot be changed unless a card ability instructs otherwise. A discard pile of zero cards cannot be shuffled back into a deck.

---

## DOUBLE-SIDED CARD

A card is double-sided if **neither of its sides has a card back**.

- Would enter an out-of-play area other than victory display or set-aside area → **removed from the game**.
- If it has "Standard Mode Only" and "Expert Mode Only" sides: expert mode → "Expert Mode Only" faceup; otherwise → "Standard Mode Only" faceup.

---

## DRAW / DRAWING CARDS

Cards drawn from the **top** of a player's deck, one at a time. Drawn cards added to the player's **hand**.

**Hand Size:** Checked at the **end of the player phase**; if over hand size, discard down to hand size. When drawing up to hand size: draw one at a time, checking after each card.

---

## ENCOUNTER CARD

Eight encounter card types: **Attachment, Environment, Main Scheme, Minion, Obligation, Side Scheme, Treachery, Villain**. Most encounter cards have an **orange** card back.

---

## ENCOUNTER DECK

Contains the attachments, environments, minions, side schemes, and treacheries players face during a scenario.

| Situation                                                          | Result                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Encounter deck empties                                             | Immediately shuffle discard pile into a new deck; place 1 acceleration token next to main scheme deck |
| Both deck AND discard pile empty simultaneously                    | Infinite acceleration tokens → **players lose**                                                       |
| Card ability discards X cards from encounter deck and deck empties | Ability is considered fulfilled; do NOT continue with newly shuffled deck                             |

---

## ENCOUNTER SET

A grouping of encounter cards. Four types: scenario-specific, modular encounter sets, Standard set, Expert set.

- Encounter sets with the **same name but different set icons** are **distinct sets**.

---

## ENEMY

A minion or villain. When used as a descriptor, "enemy" refers to game elements belonging to the scenario.

---

## ENGAGE

When a minion enters play in a player's play area, it **engages** that player.

- Unless otherwise specified, the minion engages the player **resolving the current encounter card**.
- An engaged minion remains engaged until **defeated, removed from play**, or a card ability causes it to engage another player.
- A minion **cannot be caused to engage the same player again** while already engaged with them.

---

## ENTERS PLAY

Any time a card transitions from an out-of-play area **into play**. Methods: playing a card, putting a card into play via a card ability, revealing a card from the encounter deck.

---

## ENVIRONMENT

An encounter card type that creates an overarching rule or set of rules for the scenario. Enters play in the **villain's play area**. Remains until a card ability or game effect causes it to leave play. Environment cards are considered to be in **all players' game areas** (relevant for scenarios where players are split into separate game areas).

---

## EVENT

A player card type played for an **instantaneous effect**.

- Player places it faceup on the table (not in play), pays costs, resolves effects, then places it in the owner's discard pile.
- If an event has **more than one triggered ability**: the player chooses one to trigger when playing it.
- If effects are **canceled**: card is still considered played; costs remain paid.
- An event card cannot be played if it requires one or more targets with **none valid**.
- Event cards are considered an **extension of an identity**. Attacks, thwarts, defenses, action abilities, and triggered abilities that resolve from events are also considered performed by the player's identity.
- If an effect modifies the amount of damage an event deals or threat it removes and the event deals/removes **multiple instances**: each instance is modified.
  - Exception: if an effect modifies damage "an attack" deals (not "an event"), and the event initiates multiple attacks: only the **first** attack is modified.

---

## EXCESS DAMAGE

Any amount of damage dealt to a character **beyond** that character's remaining hit points.

---

## EXHAUSTED

A card is exhausted when **rotated 90 degrees**.

- Cannot be exhausted again until **readied**.
- A card ability on an exhausted card is active and can interact with the game state; however, if the card must exhaust to pay an ability's cost, that ability **cannot be used** until the card is ready.

---

## EXPERT SET / EXPERT MODE

The Expert set is added to scenarios during **expert mode**. It is **not** a modular encounter set and cannot be selected when a scenario requires choosing a modular encounter set.

**Expert Mode:** Use the listed expert mode villain stages and add the Expert encounter set. Can be combined with heroic mode, skirmish mode, and/or campaign mode.

---

## FIND

When instructed to find a card, search **each game area** where that card could be found (play area, set-aside area, player deck, discard pile, encounter deck, etc.).

**Exceptions — areas NOT searched:**

- Facedown encounter cards in any in-play game area.
- The victory display.
- Cards removed from the game.

If instructed to "find and reveal" a **minion already in play**: that player engages that minion and resolves any keywords/triggered abilities as if that minion were being revealed. That minion retains attachments and tokens, is **not** considered to be entering play, and is considered to engage that player unless already engaged.

## FLIP

When instructed to flip a card, turn it upside down so the face that was faceup is now facedown.

- Same card type as previous face: retains attached cards, tucked cards, status cards, and tokens.
- Different card type from previous face: all attachments, status cards, and tokens are **discarded**.

---

## "FOR EACH"

Indicates an effect is repeated based on the number of a countable game element.

- **Without "choose"**: applies to a single target; counts as a single instance of damage/threat.
- **With "choose"**: each iteration is a **separate instance**. Game state updates after each. Responses can trigger after each instance. Modifiers to "for each" effects apply to **each instance**.

---

## FORCED

A bold trigger word making initiation **mandatory**.

- Forced Interrupt/Response: must resolve when their triggering conditions are met.
- Forced Action: must resolve before the player phase can end; can trigger at any valid action timing.
- If a forced ability has **no valid targets**: it does **not** initiate (costs are not paid).
- For a given triggering condition: forced abilities initiate **before** non-forced abilities of the same type.
- If two or more forced abilities would initiate simultaneously: the **first player** determines the order.
- Each forced ability must **resolve as completely as possible** before the next forced ability triggered by the same condition initiates.

---

## FORM / CHANGE FORM

A player can be in **hero form** or **alter-ego form** at any given time (indicated by identity card).

- Once each round, during their turn, each player may change form by flipping their identity card. **Once per turn only.**
- When changing form: only the form changes. Character retains sustained damage, status cards, lasting effects, attachments, tokens, and current state (ready or exhausted).
- A **card ability** causing a player to change forms does **not** count against the once-per-turn limit.
- While in **hero form**: card abilities interacting with the alter-ego do **not** interact with the identity.
- While in **alter-ego form**: card abilities interacting with the hero do **not** interact with the identity.
- Additional forms (from the "form" keyword): come with their own conditions for changing into them; changing does not count against the once-per-turn flip limit, but **does** count as changing form for triggering card effects.

---

## GAME ELEMENT

A component or person involved in playing a game: Cards, Decks, Discard Piles, Hands (of cards), Hit Point Dials, Players, Tokens.

## HIT POINTS

Each character has a hit point value representing their durability.

**Identity/Villain:** Tracked by a **hit point dial**. Dial reaches 0 → defeated.

- "Gets +X hit points": increase the dial by X. When the ability ceases: reduce the dial by X.

**Ally/Minion:** Tracked by **damage tokens**. 0 or fewer remaining HP → defeated and discarded.

- "Gets +X hit points" ceases while the character has damage ≥ its HP → the character is **defeated**.

**Infinite hit points:** Cannot be defeated by taking damage; damage may still be dealt to them.

**Starting hit points:** Refers to an identity's **printed** hit point value.

**Remaining hit points:** Identity/Villain — the dial value. Ally/Minion — maximum HP (printed value ± modifiers) minus sustained damage (total damage tokens).

**Maximum hit points:** Base hit points plus or minus all "gets" hit point modifiers active on that character.

## IDENTITY

A player card type representing which character a player is playing.

- A **double-sided card**: hero on one side, alter-ego on the other.
- Each player **begins the game in alter-ego form**.
- If a card refers to a hero or alter-ego by title: refers **only to the identity with that title**, not the other side.
- Identity cards **cannot be discarded** from play.
- Faceup side is **in play**; facedown side is **out of play**.

---

## IDENTITY-SPECIFIC CARD

Cards belonging to an identity's accompanying set. Deck **must include each** identity-specific card in the **exact quantities** specified. May only be used alongside an identity if they **share a set icon** with that identity. Identity cards are themselves identity-specific cards.

---

## IGNORE

An ability that ignores some ability, icon, or cost treats it as **not in effect or not present** while that ability is resolving.

- Played "ignoring its resource cost": no resources paid; considered played with **zero resources paid** for card effect purposes.

---

## IN PLAY AND OUT OF PLAY

**In play:** faceup identity card, faceup ally/support/upgrade cards that have entered play, faceup top card of villain deck and main scheme deck, faceup attachment/environment/minion/obligation/side scheme cards that have entered play.

**Out of play:** cards in a player's hand, deck, and discard pile; cards in the encounter deck, encounter discard pile, unrevealed villain/main scheme cards, and facedown encounter cards dealt to a player; facedown cards attached to in-play cards; cards removed from the game or set aside.

**Key rules:**

- Card abilities only interact with and can only target **in-play cards** (unless the ability text specifically refers to an out-of-play area).
- A card **enters play** when it moves from out-of-play to a play area.
- A card **leaves play** when it moves from a play area to out-of-play.

---

## INDIRECT DAMAGE

- Indirect damage dealt to a **player**: divided as that player chooses among characters under their control.
- Indirect damage dealt to a **group of players**: divided as the group chooses among friendly characters in play.
- All indirect damage from a single source is **first assigned, then resolved simultaneously**.
  - Cannot be assigned more indirect damage than would defeat a character.
  - A character with a **tough status card** can be assigned indirect damage up to its remaining hit points; all assigned damage is then prevented by the tough status card.
- Characters that **cannot take damage** cannot be assigned indirect damage.
  - If indirect damage cannot be assigned to any character the player controls: that damage is **ignored**.
- If an enemy attack deals indirect damage: dealt during **step 4 of the enemy activation** (after defenders are declared). Only the defending character (or attacked player's identity if undefended) is considered to have been attacked.

---

## INITIATING ABILITIES

When a player wishes to play a card or initiate a triggered ability:

**Check first:**

1. Can the card be played or ability initiated at this time? Does it have at least one valid target?
2. Can the player pay the costs (including modifiers)? If card has resource cost X, player chooses X here.

**If both checks pass, follow these steps:** 3. If playing a card: place it faceup on the table (not in play). 4. Apply any modifiers to the cost(s). 5. Pay the cost(s). If costs cannot be paid at this step, **abort without paying any costs**. 6. The card commences being played, or the effects of the ability attempt to initiate. 7. The card is played or the ability resolves. The card enters play (or, if event, effects resolve and it's placed in discard pile).

**Additional rules:**

- If any step would make an **interrupt** ability's triggering condition true: that ability may be initiated just before that condition becomes true.
- If any step would make a **response** ability's triggering condition true: that ability may be initiated immediately after that condition becomes true.
- If the ability being initiated is on an **in-play card**: the sequence does not stop if that card leaves play unless leaving play **prevents a required cost from being paid**.

---

## INTERRUPT

A triggered ability indicated by the bold "**Interrupt**" timing trigger. Resolves **immediately before** its triggering condition resolves.

- Players can only trigger on **cards they control** or **encounter cards** (not obligations in other players' play areas).
- Each interrupt can only be triggered **once per occurrence** of its triggering condition.
- Multiple copies of the same card can each trigger on the same condition.
- Interrupts using **"would"** resolve before the triggering condition **initiates**.
- If an interrupt **changes or cancels** the imminent triggering condition: **no further interrupts** to the original condition can be triggered.
- Once all players decline to use further interrupts: that interrupt window is **closed**.
- First player has **first opportunity** to use an interrupt; then proceeds clockwise.
- Forced interrupts initiate **before** non-forced interrupts for the same condition.

## LABELED ABILITY

A triggered ability with a parenthetical after its bold trigger text designating it as **(attack)**, **(defense)**, and/or **(thwart)**.

- **(attack)**: considered an attack made by the player's identity.
- **(defense)**: considered a defense made by the player's identity. When initiated during an attack, the player's identity becomes the defender.
- **(thwart)**: considered a thwart made by the player's identity.
- **Multiple labels**: considered to be each of the labeled types.
- If the player's identity has **one or more status cards that cancel any of the labeled types**: the entire ability (except costs) is **canceled**. The identity is not considered to have attacked/defended/thwarted. Each relevant status card is removed when the ability is canceled.

---

## LASTING EFFECTS

Card abilities creating effects or conditions for a specified duration ("until end of phase," etc.).

- Persists beyond the resolution of the ability that created it, for the **specified duration** (whether or not the creating card is still in play).
- Treated as a **constant ability** with the same timing priority.
- **Updates** whenever the game state updates.
- If a card enters play after a lasting effect is created: it is **still affected** by that lasting effect.
- **Expires** as soon as the specified timing point is reached. "Until end of round" expires just before "at end of round" abilities or delayed effects initiate.
- Can only be **initiated during** the time period it covers.

---

## LEAVES PLAY

Any time a card transitions from an in-play area to an out-of-play area.

- When a card leaves play: **no memory of its previous state**; considered a new copy.
- When a card leaves play:
  - Discard each card **attached to or tucked under** that card.
  - Discard each **boost card** given to that card.
  - Return each **token and status card** on that card to the supply.

---

## LIMIT

"Limit X per [period]" — card-specific. Each copy of an ability may be used X times per the specified period, per instance. If an effect with a limit is **canceled**: the card is still considered played/initiated, and it **counts toward the limit**.

## LOOK / LOOKED-AT

If instructed to look at otherwise-hidden cards: only the **resolving player** can look. That player may convey any amount of information to other players. While looking at cards in a deck, those cards are **still considered part of that deck**. After looking, cards are returned in the **same order**.

---

## MAIN SCHEME / MAIN SCHEME DECK

Main scheme is an encounter card type representing the villain's **primary objective**.

**Threat placement (step 1 of villain phase):** Place threat equal to the acceleration field (bottom-right corner). All active acceleration tokens and icons each add 1 additional threat.

**Completing a stage:** If threat on main scheme ≥ its target threat value → the main scheme is **completed** and the main scheme deck **advances**.

- **If the villain completes the final stage: the villain wins the game.**
- If the main scheme advances by means other than accumulated threat: it is **not** considered completed.

**When the main scheme deck advances:**

1. Remove the top main scheme card from the game. Return all tokens (except acceleration tokens) to the token pool; discard each attached card.
2. Resolve any "When Revealed" ability on the **"A" side** of the new top card.
3. Flip the new top card to its "**B side**," place threat equal to its starting threat value, and resolve any "When Revealed" ability on that side.

- Excess threat from the previous stage **does not carry over**.
- Acceleration tokens **do carry over**.
- Main scheme cards **cannot be discarded** from play.

---

## MAX / MAXIMUM

"Max" and "maximum" impose a maximum **across all copies of a card (by title) for all players**.

| Format                             | Effect                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Max X per [period]                 | Maximum times copies can be played during the period; canceled cards count                                     |
| Max X per deck                     | Restricts copies per player deck                                                                               |
| Max 1 per player                   | Player-specific; restricts copies in play per player; cannot take control of another copy they already control |
| Max 1 per [game element]           | Restricts copies attached to each indicated game element                                                       |
| Max 1 per [instance]               | Restricts times ability can be triggered by a single instance across all copies                                |
| (to a maximum of +X)               | Applies only to that ability; multiple abilities with this maximum can stack beyond +X                         |
| (to a maximum [game element] of X) | Applies across all abilities that increase that game element                                                   |

---

## MINION

An encounter card type representing supporters of the villain.

- When a minion enters play: engages the player **revealing the card** or resolving the ability that put it into play (unless an ability specifies otherwise).
- Remains in play until a card ability or game effect causes it to leave play.
- 0 or fewer remaining HP: **defeated and discarded**.
- Engaged minions activate during **step 2 of the villain phase**, after the villain activates, one at a time in the engaged player's choice of order.
- If a minion engages a player during an enemy activation in which all engaged minions are instructed to activate (e.g., a "Boost" ability during step 2): the **newly-engaged minion will also activate**.

## MODES OF PLAY

| Mode                  | Description                                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Standard**          | Basic mode; follow the scenario's content and setup instructions                                                           |
| **Expert**            | Use expert mode villain stages; add Expert encounter set to encounter deck                                                 |
| **Heroic**            | Choose a heroic level (e.g., 1 or 4); deal X additional encounter cards to each player during step 3 of each villain phase |
| **Skirmish / Rookie** | Choose any one version of the villain; put only that version into play; remove each other version from the game            |
| **Campaign**          | Interconnected scenarios played one after another; rules in the campaign's associated rulebook                             |

All modes (except Standard) can be combined with each other. Increasing a campaign's difficulty does not lock players into any other specific mode.

## MODIFIERS

The game **constantly checks and updates** any variable quantity being modified.

- Any time a new modifier is applied or removed: the **entire quantity is recalculated** from the start.
- The **per-player icon (👤)** is **not** a modifier; applied before any modifiers.
- All modifiers are calculated as if applied simultaneously. **Additive/subtractive modifiers are calculated before doubling/halving modifiers.**
- If a value is "set" to a specific number: the **set modifier overrides** all non-set modifiers. If multiple set modifiers conflict: the **most recently resolved** set modifier takes precedence.
- After all modifiers: if a value is **below zero**, it is treated as **zero**.
- **Fractional values are rounded up** after all modifiers are applied.
- A value of **dash (–) cannot be modified**.

---

## MOVE

Some abilities allow players to move game elements (cards, damage, or threat).

- An element **cannot move to its same current placement**.
- If no valid source or destination: the move **cannot be made**.
- Damage moved **off** a character: considered **healed** from that character.
- Damage moved **to** a character: considered **dealt** to that character.
- Threat moved **off** a scheme: considered **removed** from that scheme.
- Threat moved **to** a scheme: considered **placed** on that scheme.

---

## NEMESIS ENCOUNTER SET

Each identity comes with an associated nemesis encounter set, **set aside out of play** at the start of the game. Nemesis cards may only be used alongside an identity if they **share a set icon** with that identity.

- "Nemesis minion": the minion belonging to that identity's nemesis set.
- "Nemesis side scheme": the side scheme belonging to that identity's nemesis set.

---

## OBLIGATION

An encounter card type representing a commitment or obstacle for an identity's alter-ego.

- Abilities using "you"/"your" apply **only to the player whose play area the obligation is in**.
- Each identity's associated obligation cards are shuffled into the **encounter deck** during setup.
- If an obligation is revealed that must be given to a specific player: place it in that player's play area. If it cannot be given to the specified player: **ignore its ability, remove it from the game, and reveal an additional encounter card**.
- If an obligation is revealed without a specified player: the revealing player places it in **their** play area.
- Only the player with the obligation can trigger abilities or pay costs on it.
- If a player **draws** an obligation from their player deck: place it in their play area. Does not draw a replacement card unless refilling to hand size.

---

## "OTHERWISE"

Effects beginning with "otherwise" resolve **only if the preceding effect was not resolved**. Resolves if the preceding effect has a condition that is not true, or has an effect that cannot at least partially resolve.

**Scope:**

- Preceded by a **semicolon**: the "preceding effect" refers to effects before the semicolon in the same sentence.
- **Its own sentence**: the "preceding effect" refers to the sentence **directly before** the "otherwise" sentence.
