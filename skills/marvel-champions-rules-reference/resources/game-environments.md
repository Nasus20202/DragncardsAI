# Marvel Champions — Game Environments (Beta)

**Source:** Rules Reference v1.7 | **Referenced by:** SKILL.md

---

## OVERVIEW

When preparing for a game, a group of players may choose a **game environment** for that game, or allow each player to choose their own environment independently.

The game environment establishes **which Marvel Champions products can be used** during player deck customization for that game.

**Key rules:**

- A player customizes their deck using **only player cards** included in the products designated by their environment.
- This includes the choice of **which hero to play as**.
- A group may choose **any scenario with any modular encounter sets**, regardless of the chosen environment(s). Environments only restrict **player deck** customization.

There are three types of environments: **Current**, **Legacy**, and **Limited**.

---

## CURRENT ENVIRONMENT

Consists of the **most recent products** released for Marvel Champions, in addition to the core set. Designed to keep the card pool focused on the newest content.

**Products included as of v1.7 (Dec 2025):**

**Core Set:**

- Marvel Champions: The Card Game core set

**Expansions:**

- The Age of Apocalypse campaign expansion
- The Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. campaign expansion
- The Civil War custom scenario expansion

**Hero Packs:**

- Iceman Hero Pack
- Jubilee Hero Pack
- Nightcrawler Hero Pack
- Magneto Hero Pack
- Black Panther Hero Pack
- Silk Hero Pack
- Falcon Hero Pack
- Winter Soldier Hero Pack
- Wonder Man Hero Pack
- Hercules Hero Pack

**Also includes:** All products released since v1.7 was published (Dec 2025).

---

## LEGACY ENVIRONMENT

Consists of **all Marvel Champions products**. A player customizes their deck using player cards from **any product in their collection**. No restrictions on which products can be used.

---

## LIMITED ENVIRONMENT

Allows a player who owns a number of older products to continue playing with those products while still reaping the benefits of a smaller, more focused card pool.

**Products allowed in a Limited Environment:**

- The Marvel Champions: The Card Game core set
- Up to **3 campaign and/or custom scenario expansions**
- Up to **10 hero packs**

Any expansion or hero pack may be chosen. Finding creative combinations of products can be an enjoyable deckbuilding experience.

## SUMMARY TABLE

| Environment | Card Pool                                           | Best For                                                               |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Current** | Core set + most recent wave(s)                      | Players who want to focus on the newest cards                          |
| **Legacy**  | All products ever released                          | Players who want maximum variety and access to every card              |
| **Limited** | Core set + up to 3 expansions + up to 10 hero packs | Players who own specific products and want a focused, synergistic pool |
