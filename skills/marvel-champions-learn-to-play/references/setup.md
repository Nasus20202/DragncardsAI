# Setup — The 14 Setup Steps

Covers getting a scenario onto the table, and the expert-mode setup change.

**Referenced by:** SKILL.md | **Authoritative version:** `marvel-champions-rules-reference/resources/setup.md` (16 steps)

---

Perform the following 14 steps in order.

### Step 1 — Select Heroes

- Each player selects one **identity card** and places it **alter-ego side face up**.

### Step 2 — Set Hit Points

- Set each player's hit points to the **starting hit points** listed at the bottom of their identity card.

### Step 3 — Select First Player

- Players agree on a first player and place the **first player token** in front of them.

### Step 4 — Set Aside Obligations

- Remove each hero's **obligation card** from their deck and set it aside (used later in setup).

### Step 5 — Set Aside Nemesis Sets

- Remove each hero's **nemesis cards** from their deck and set them aside. They do not start in play but may enter play via card abilities.
- Nemesis cards **share a common label**.

### Step 6 — Shuffle Player Decks

- Each player shuffles their player deck and places it near their identity card.
- **Do not shuffle the title card** into the deck (decklists are on the back).

### Step 7 — Collect Tokens and Status Cards

- Create a shared pool of **damage tokens**, **threat tokens**, and **all-purpose counters**.
- Place stacks of **Stunned**, **Confused**, and **Tough** status cards nearby.

### Step 8 — Select Villain

- Place the villain deck and main scheme deck in the **center of the play area**.
- Stack villain cards in sequential order (Stage I on top, final stage on bottom).

### Step 9 — Set Villain's Hit Points

- Set the villain's hit points to the value on the current villain card **× number of players** (👤 symbol).

### Step 10 — Resolve Scheme Setup

- Resolve any **"Setup"** instructions on the main scheme card.

### Step 11 — Create and Shuffle Encounter Deck

- Shuffle the obligation cards (from Step 4) into the villain's encounter deck.
- Nemesis cards **remain set aside** until called for by an encounter card.

### Step 12 — Draw Cards

- Each player draws cards equal to their **hand size** (listed at the bottom-left of the identity card).

### Step 13 — Resolve Mulligans

- Each player may **discard any number of cards** from their hand, then draw back up to their starting hand size.
- Do **not** shuffle discarded cards back into the deck at this time.

### Step 14 — Resolve Character Setup Abilities

- Resolve any **"Setup"** instructions on identity cards in play.

---

## Expert Mode

- Use the expert villain stage (III) and add the **expert encounter set**.
- The expert set is **not** a modular encounter set and cannot be chosen when a scenario asks players to select a modular set.

For the other modes of play (heroic, skirmish, campaign) and how they combine, load
`marvel-champions-rules-reference/resources/modes.md`.
